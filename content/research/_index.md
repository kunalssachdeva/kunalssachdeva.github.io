My research asks how firms and financial markets respond to environmental regulation and climate risk — and whether those responses are real. Recent work studies whether shareholder campaigns actually cut pollution, whether large corporations report their emissions accurately, and how credit and corporate governance shape what firms do. Related projects span financial intermediation, household finance, and sustainable investing.

I referee for the *Journal of Finance*, the *Review of Financial Studies*, the *Journal of Financial Economics*, *Management Science*, the *Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis*, and the *Review of Finance*, among other finance and economics journals.
